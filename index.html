How Lucidia works

🧠

Persistent Memory

Every conversation, every preference, every project detail is stored locally in a memory ledger. When you come back tomorrow, Lucidia knows exactly where you left off. No re-explaining.

🔒

Your Data Stays Yours

Lucidia runs on your hardware. Your conversations never leave your network. No cloud training, no data harvesting, no surveillance. You own everything.

📐

Actually Teaches

Not just answers — understanding. Lucidia uses the Concrete-Representational-Abstract framework to build genuine conceptual knowledge. She adapts to how you learn.

🔍

Search That Works

Semantic search across your documents, conversations, and projects. Find anything you've ever discussed. RAG-powered retrieval with local vector embeddings.

⚡

Fast, Local Inference

Powered by Ollama running on your own machine. No API keys, no rate limits, no monthly token caps. Works offline. Responds instantly.

🌍

Multi-Agent Ecosystem

Lucidia isn't alone. She coordinates with specialized agents — a math tutor, a code reviewer, a writing coach, a research assistant — each with deep domain knowledge.
